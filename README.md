# AI-Teachers
This repository accompanies the paper "Spotted, Then Rewarded: Congolese Teacher Educators Recognize AI-Written Answers, Grade Them Higher, and Rank the Assessment Formats They Would Trust", submitted to the International Workshop on Applied AI &amp; Mathematical Methods for Real-world Applications (A2MRA), April 13–15, 2027, Delft, Netherlands.

The study surveyed 41 teacher educators from three Congolese institutions (ISP/Mbanza-Ngungu, ISP/Gombe and UPN). Each read six answers to the same examination question — three written by second-year students, three generated by a chatbot — judged their source and gave a mark out of 20. Teachers also ranked six assessment formats on four criteria. The paper reports detection sensitivity, the effect of suspicion on marks, and an MMCM aggregation of the format rankings.
